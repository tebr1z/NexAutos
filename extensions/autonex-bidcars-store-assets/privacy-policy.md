# Auto Nex – BidCars Final Price Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The extension reads public vehicle and price information from supported BidCars pages opened by the user. It sends the lot URL and vehicle calculation fields to the configured Auto Nex API to obtain auction, transport and customs estimates. It does not collect passwords, payment-card data, private messages or browsing history outside supported pages.

Extension settings and locally learned transport tariffs are stored using Chrome extension storage. Users can remove this data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage. The extension is not affiliated with BidCars, Copart or IAAI.
